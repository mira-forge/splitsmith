# Trip planning

Ten days, three cities, one carry-on each. Everything below came out of a single long brain dump.
Costs are in [[#Budget|the budget]], and the [Sintra tip](#^sintra) matters most.

## Lisbon

- Stay in Alfama, near the Miradouro de Santa Luzia.
- Tram 28 early in the morning, before the queues. Back to [[#Lisbon]] notes, on to [[#Porto]].
- Day trip to Sintra: book Pena Palace tickets ahead. ^sintra

## Porto

Train from Santa Apolónia, about three hours.

- Walk across the Dom Luís I bridge at sunset.
- Port cellar tour in Vila Nova de Gaia.
- Route map: ![route](assets/route.png), tickets in [[Trip planning#Budget]].
